# Security policy

This policy covers every repository in the Mnemosyne Research Labs organization, unless a repository has its own `SECURITY.md`.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email hello@mnemosyneresearch.com with "Security" in the subject. Please don't open a public issue. Include what you found, where, and the steps to reproduce it.

We will:

- acknowledge your report within 3 business days
- triage it within 10 business days and tell you what we found
- fix critical issues within 30 days and high-severity issues within 90
- tell you when it is fixed and credit you, if you want to be credited

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith under this policy, we will not take legal action against you or ask anyone else to, and we will treat your research as authorized. Good faith means you:

- report the issue to us first and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you disclose it
- access only the data you need to show the issue, and don't keep, change or share it
- don't degrade our services or anyone's use of them, and don't use social engineering or physical attacks

If you are unsure whether something is in scope, ask us first.
